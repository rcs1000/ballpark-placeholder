How it works

  1. 1

    Enter the risk once

    ZIP, a driver or two, a vehicle, and a coverage bundle. Aggressive defaults — a minimum quote needs only the essentials.

  2. 2

    We rate in parallel

    Your risk fans out to the carriers you're appointed with. Results stream in as each one responds — a slow carrier never holds up the rest.

  3. 3

    Compare and verify

    Ranked estimates with confidence bands, plus which carriers declined and why. Pick the winner, then confirm the real rate before you bind.

Straight talk: Ballpark gives you estimated indications from public filings and modeled data — not bindable carrier rates. It's the fastest way to know where a risk lands before you do the real work. Always verify with the carrier before binding.
